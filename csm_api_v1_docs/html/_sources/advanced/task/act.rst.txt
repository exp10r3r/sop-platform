扫描任务控制
================

用于控制扫描任务启停、重新扫描等

Request
~~~~~~~~

请求地址
+++++++++

.. code-block:: bash

   POST /api/v1/task_manage/act/{id}



请求参数
+++++++++++++++++++++

+--------+-----------+----------+-----------------------------+------+
| 字段   | 字段名称  | 是否必填 | 说明                        | 示例 |
+========+===========+==========+=============================+======+
|| type  || 操作类型 || 是      || doit：任务启停             ||     |
||       ||          ||         || priority：设置优先         ||     |
||       ||          ||         || reset_scan：重新扫描       ||     |
||       ||          ||         || del_data：删除任务关联数据 ||     |
||       ||          ||         ||                            ||     |
+--------+-----------+----------+-----------------------------+------+
|| value || 操作值   || 是      || doit：                     ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 0暂停 1启动                ||     |
||       ||          ||         ||                            ||     |
||       ||          ||         || priority：                 ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 0非优先 1优先              ||     |
||       ||          ||         ||                            ||     |
||       ||          ||         || reset_scan：               ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 基础模式：                 ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 0：端口服务                ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 2：OS识别                  ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 4：插件扫描                ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 主域模式：                 ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 0：子域名收集              ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 12：端口探测               ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 14：站点指纹识别           ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 2：OS识别                  ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 4：插件扫描                ||     |
||       ||          ||         ||                            ||     |
||       ||          ||         || del_data：                 ||     |
||       ||          ||         || 1删除                      ||     |
+--------+-----------+----------+-----------------------------+------+


请求示例
+++++++++
   
.. code-block:: python

    import requests
    import json

    url = "http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/v1/task_manage/act/4490"

    payload = json.dumps({
        "type": "del_data",
        "value": 1
    })
    headers = {
        'Accept': 'application/json',
        'Content-type': 'application/json',
        'ACCESSKEY': 'ijE80piSATBYT8qVQcInSygUomWl6FiQFZ9LwhdZBv0eJ6QQFHQcHw',
        'SECRETKEY': 'P4KNH51u6KyewjCfX1JZ3uZ7bCV4tTME6JcbBvmrUQTZ7YxOltUjCIiNUrp24ijq8'
    }

    response = requests.request("POST", url, headers=headers, data=payload)

    print(response.text)

Response
~~~~~~~~

响应参数
++++++++++++

=============   ===============   ===============
参数             类型             描述
=============   ===============   ===============
status          boolean           是否成功
info            dict/string       返回信息
=============   ===============   ===============


示例值
+++++++++

.. code-block:: javascript

    {
        "info": "操作成功",
        "status": true
    }


失败示例
+++++++++

.. code-block:: javascript

    {
        "info": {
            "detail": "Not found."
        },
        "status": false
    }

创建任务
================

用于创建扫描任务

Request
~~~~~~~~

请求地址
+++++++++

.. code-block:: bash

   POST /api/v1/task_manage



请求参数
+++++++++++++++++++++

| 字段                 | 字段名称                   | 是否必填 | 说明                                                                                                                    | 示例                                |

||                     ||                           ||         || customize：自定义端口                                                                                                  ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || self-adaption：自适应端口                                                                                              ||                                    |

|| value_type          || 扫描目标范围              || 是      || customize：自定义                                                                                                      ||                                    |

||                     ||                           ||         || cycle为2时:                                                                                                            ||                                    |

||                     ||                           ||         || week:每周                                                                                                              ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || month:每月                                                                                                             ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         ||                                                                                                                        ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 不传时默认值为 day                                                                                                     ||                                    |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
|| cycle_start         || 扫描开始阶段              || 否      || scan_type为base时,可选值为:                                                                                            ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 0 端口扫描                                                                                                             ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 2 OS识别                                                                                                               ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 4 插件扫描                                                                                                             ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         ||                                                                                                                        ||                                    |

||                     ||                           ||         || 12 端口探测                                                                                                            ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 14 站点指纹识别                                                                                                        ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 2 OS识别                                                                                                               ||                                    |

+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
| cycle_scantime       | 禁止扫描时间模板id         | 否       |                                                                                                                         |                                     |

||                     ||                           ||         || 0：否                                                                                                                  ||                                    |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
|| osscan_mode         || 是否进行操作系统识别      || 否      || 不传时默认值为1                                                                                                        ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 1：是                                                                                                                  ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 0：否                                                                                                                  ||                                    |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
| task_start_time      | 任务开始时间               | 否       | 例如：2024-05-22 17:23:13                                                                                               |                                     |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
|| plugin_type         || 插件模板值类型            || 否      || type：按插件类型指定PoC插件                                                                                            ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || level：按漏洞等级指定PoC插件                                                                                           ||                                    |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
|| plugin_value        || 插件模板值                ||         || 当plugin_type为type时，可选值有：                                                                                      ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || CMS-WordPress、None、缓冲区溢出、权限绕过、CMS-other、默认口令、目录遍历、XML实体注入、                                ||                                    |
||                     ||                           || 否      || 弱口令、文件包含、未授权访问、用户枚举、github、URL重定向、目录浏览、CMS-Discuz、                                      ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 任意文件读取、远程代码执行、用友、跨站脚本攻击(XSS)、SSRF服务端请求伪造、海康、                                        ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 后门/backdoor、DOS、站点指纹、敏感信息泄露、SQL注入、文件上传、设计缺陷、2021HW漏洞、CMS-DedeCms、平行权限、CMS-PHPCMS ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         ||                                                                                                                        ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 当plugin_type为level时，可选值有：                                                                                     ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 严重、高危、中危、低危                                                                                                 ||                                    |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
| plugin_id            | 插件id                     | 否       | 用于额外指定PoC插件id                                                                                                   |                                     |
+----------------------+----------------------------+----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
|| taskcfg             || 第三方扫描任务-扫描器配置 || 否      || scan_type 为 third_scan时必填                                                                                          ||                                    |
||                     ||                           ||         || 通常为可配置的自定义规则，在「系统设置」「平台联动」「第三方引擎配置」中进行配置                                       || {                                  |
||                     ||                           ||         || 配置完成后，此字段的需要提交的数据的校验规则可以使用接口 :doc:`「扫描器配置字段详情」 <./third_config>` 获取           || "type": "tenablesc",               |
||                     ||                           ||         ||                                                                                                                        || "config": [                        |
||                     ||                           ||         || 字段格式为字典格式，包含的内容有扫描器类型、扫描器列表                                                                 || {                                  |
||                     ||                           ||         ||                                                                                                                        || "collector_id": 144,               |
||                     ||                           ||         || type 为字符串格式表示要调用扫描的扫描器类型                                                                            || "policy_id": "CVM_FULL_PLUGI111NS" |

.. note:: plugin_id、plugin_type、plugin_value 仅在scanids字段为空时生效，用于选择插件并生成一个新的模板（该模板仅当前任务可用）

    import requests
    import json

    url = "http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/v1/task_manage"

    payload = json.dumps({
        "value": "192.168.1.1",
        "zone_name": "default",
        "port_type": "customize",
        "portargv": "90,99",
        "name": "api添加测试",
        "cycle_interval": 9,
        "cycle_interval_type": "day",
        "portdeep": 1,
        "cycle": 0,
        "cpescan": 1,
        "task_start_time": "2023-09-09 15:12:41",
        "scan_type": "base",
        "value_type": "customize",
        "scanids": None,
        "cycle_scantime": None,
        "plugin_type": "type",
        "plugin_value": [
            "目录遍历",
            "文件上传"
        ],
        "plugin_id": [
            1,
            2,
            3,
            4,
            5
        ]
    })
    headers = {
        'Accept': 'application/json',
        'Content-type': 'application/json',
        'ACCESSKEY': 'ijE80piSATBYT8qVQcInSygUomWl6FiQFZ9LwhdZBv0eJ6QQFHQcHw',
        'SECRETKEY': 'P4KNH51u6KyewjCfX1JZ3uZ7bCV4tTME6JcbBvmrUQTZ7YxOltUjCIiNUrp24ijq8'
    }

    response = requests.request("POST", url, headers=headers, data=payload)

    print(response.text)

    import requests
    import json

    url = "http://127.0.0.1:38202/api/v1/task_manage"

    payload = json.dumps({
    "scan_type": "thirdscan",
    "value": "192.168.1.1",

        "config": [
        {
            "collector_id": 144,

    'Content-Type': 'application/json',

编辑模板
==============================================================

用于编辑已有的导出数据模板

Request
~~~~~~~~

请求地址
+++++++++

.. code-block:: bash

   PUT /api/v1/report/data/config/{id}



请求参数
+++++++++++++++++++++

+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| 字段            | 字段名称                         | 是否必填 | 说明                                                                                             | 示例 |
+=================+==================================+==========+==================================================================================================+======+
| name            | 导出任务名称                     | 是       |                                                                                                  |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| type            | Excel版本                        | 是       | xlsx或xls                                                                                        |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| task_ids        | 关联任务ID                       | 否       | 数组格式                                                                                         |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| group_ids       | 关联资产组ID                     | 否       | 数组格式                                                                                         |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
|| condition      || 需要导出的数据表名              || 是      || 数组格式                                                                                        ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         ||                                                                                                 ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || ip: IP资产                                                                                      ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || task: 扫描任务                                                                                  ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || assets_group: 资产组                                                                            ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || domain: 域名资产                                                                                ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || port: 端口服务                                                                                  ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || poc: PoC漏洞                                                                                    ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || version: 版本型漏洞                                                                             ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || riskcollector: 第三方漏洞                                                                       ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || riskport: 高危端口                                                                              ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || unconventional: 非常规端口                                                                      ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || riskport_rule: 高危端口-监控规则                                                                ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || conventional_rule: 常规端口-监控规则                                                            ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || riskport_white: 高危端口-白名单规则                                                             ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || webfps: 分类资产库                                                                              ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || pan: 网盘监控                                                                                   ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || wechat: 公众号小程序                                                                            ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || doc: 文档监控                                                                                   ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || git: 开源社区监控                                                                               ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || darkweb: 暗网监控                                                                               ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || wechat-keyword: 暗网监控-监控关键字                                                             ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || doc-keyword: 文档监控-监控关键字                                                                ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || pan-keyword: 网盘监控-监控关键字                                                                ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || git-keyword: 开源社区监控-监控关键字                                                            ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || darkweb-keyword: 暗网监控-监控关键字                                                            ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || net_scope: 网络组范围登记                                                                       ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || app_scope: 应用组范围登记                                                                       ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || application: APP资产监控                                                                        ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || application-keyword: APP资产监控-关键字                                                         ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || url: URL列表                                                                                    ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || shadow: 影子资产                                                                                ||     |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
|| fields         || 指定的自定义字段                || 否      || 字典格式，键为数据表名，值为指定的自定义字段                                                    ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || 为空时默认导出所有字段                                                                          ||     |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| cycle           | 是否周期任务                     | 是       | 0:否 1:是                                                                                        |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| cycle_run_time  | 周期任务在执行当日的具体执行时间 | 否       | 当cycle为1时必填，格式：09:00:00                                                                 |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| value           | 执行周期间隔                     | 是       | 单位为天，当cycle为1时生效                                                                       |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| task_start_time | 开始时间                         | 是       | 格式：2023-03-21 14:27:45                                                                        |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| system_userids  | 邮件发送对象用户id               | 否       | 数组格式                                                                                         |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| custom_email    | 邮件发送对象-系统外用户邮箱      | 否       | 字符串格式，多个邮箱使用 ``\n`` 分隔                                                             |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
|| file_type      || 文档拆分                        || 否      || user: 报告按照系统用户进行拆分（严格按照定义角色权限生成）                                      ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || user_by_group: 报告按照系统用户进行拆分（不按照定义角色权限生成，而是按照资产登记的结果来生成） ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || group：按照资产组进行拆分                                                                       ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || 空值: 不进行拆分                                                                                ||     |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+


请求示例
+++++++++
   
.. code-block:: python

    import requests
    import json

    url = "http://127.0.0.1:38202/api/v1/report/data/config/775"

    payload = json.dumps({
    "name": "tes22t222",
    "type": "xlsx",
    "task_ids": None,
    "group_ids": None,
    "condition": [
        "ip",
        "task"
    ],
    "status": 1,
    "cycle": 1,
    "cycle_run_time": "09:00:00",
    "value": "11111111",
    "task_start_time": "2024-03-21 09:11:43",
    "system_userids": None,
    "custom_email": None,
    "user_id": 1,
    "fields": {},
    "file_type": ""
    })
    headers = {
    'Content-Type': 'application/json',
    'Cookie': 'sessionid=550q0nyl2gijkeqxhu2kibep5wgdxrin'
    }

    response = requests.request("PUT", url, headers=headers, data=payload)

    print(response.text)



Response
~~~~~~~~

响应参数
++++++++++++

=============   ===============   ===============
参数             类型             描述
=============   ===============   ===============
status          boolean           是否成功
info            dict              返回数据
=============   ===============   ===============


示例值
+++++++++

.. code-block:: javascript

    {
        "info": "保存成功",
        "status": true
    }


失败示例
+++++++++

.. code-block:: javascript

    {
        "info": {
            "condition": [
                "取值范围为: dict_keys(['task', 'assets_group', 'groups', 'ip', 'domain', 'port', 'poc', 'version', 'riskport', 'riskport_rule', 'conventional_rule', 'riskport_white', 'unconventional', 'riskcollector', 'webfps', 'pan', 'wechat', 'doc', 'git', 'darkweb', 'wechat-keyword', 'doc-keyword', 'pan-keyword', 'git-keyword', 'darkweb-keyword', 'net_scope', 'app_scope', 'application', 'application-keyword', 'url', 'shadow'])"
            ]
        },
        "status": false
    }

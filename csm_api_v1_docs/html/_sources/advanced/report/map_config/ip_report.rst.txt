立即导出按IP拆分的word报告
==============================================================

用于创建新的导出报告模板

Request
~~~~~~~~

请求地址
+++++++++

.. code-block:: bash

   POST /api/v1/report/map/config/ip_report



请求参数
+++++++++++++++++++++

+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
| 字段           | 字段名称                    | 是否必填 | 说明                                          | 示例 |
+================+=============================+==========+===============================================+======+
| name           | 导出报告名称                | 是       |                                               |      |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
| task_ids       | 关联任务ID                  | 否       | 数组格式                                      |      |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
| group_ids      | 关联资产组ID                | 否       | 数组格式                                      |      |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
|| condition     || 报告中希望额外包含的数据   || 否      || 数组格式                                     ||     |
||               || 默认已包含了IP资产         ||         ||                                              ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || poc: PoC漏洞                                 ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || version: 版本型漏洞                          ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || riskcollector: 第三方漏洞                    ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || riskport: 高危端口                           ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || unconventional: 非常规端口                   ||     |
||               ||                            ||         ||                                              ||     |
||               ||                            ||         ||                                              ||     |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
| system_userids | 邮件发送对象用户id          | 否       | 数组格式                                      |      |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
| custom_email   | 邮件发送对象-系统外用户邮箱 | 否       | 字符串格式，多个邮箱使用 ``\n`` 分隔          |      |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+
|| ip_scope      || IP范围筛选                 || 否      || 支持格式:                                    ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || 192.168.1.1-192.168.1.100                    ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || 192.168.1.0/24                               ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || 192.168.1.1                                  ||     |
||               ||                            ||         || 字符串格式，多个值可使用 ``\n`` 换行符号分隔 ||     |
+----------------+-----------------------------+----------+-----------------------------------------------+------+


请求示例
+++++++++
   
.. code-block:: python

    import requests
    import json

    url = "http://127.0.0.1:38202/api/v1/report/map/config/ip_report"

    payload = json.dumps({
        "name": "test",
        "task_ids": [],
        "group_ids": [],
        "condition": ["poc", "version"],
        "system_userids": [],
        "custom_email": "",
        "ip_scope": "14.18.16.0/24\n192.168.1.1-192.168.1.100\n192.168.1.0/24"
    })
    headers = {
        'Content-Type': 'application/json',
    }

    response = requests.request("POST", url, headers=headers, data=payload)

    print(response.text)

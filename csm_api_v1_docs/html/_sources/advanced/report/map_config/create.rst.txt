创建模板
==============================================================

用于创建新的导出报告模板

Request
~~~~~~~~

请求地址
+++++++++

.. code-block:: bash

   POST /api/v1/report/map/config



请求参数
+++++++++++++++++++++

+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| 字段            | 字段名称                         | 是否必填 | 说明                                                                                             | 示例 |
+=================+==================================+==========+==================================================================================================+======+
| name            | 模板名称                         | 是       |                                                                                                  |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| type            | 报告格式                         | 是       | docx、wps或html                                                                                  |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| task_ids        | 关联任务ID                       | 否       | 数组格式                                                                                         |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| group_ids       | 关联资产组ID                     | 否       | 数组格式                                                                                         |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
|| condition      || 导出报告                        || 是      || 数组格式                                                                                        ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         ||                                                                                                 ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || ip: IP资产                                                                                      ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || task: 扫描任务                                                                                  ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || assets_group: 资产组                                                                            ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || domain: 域名资产                                                                                ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || port: 端口服务                                                                                  ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || poc: PoC漏洞                                                                                    ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || version: 版本型漏洞                                                                             ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || riskcollector: 第三方漏洞                                                                       ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || riskport: 高危端口                                                                              ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || unconventional: 非常规端口                                                                      ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || webfps: 分类资产库                                                                              ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || asset_scope: 已登记资产范围                                                                     ||     |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| cycle           | 是否周期任务                     | 是       | 0:否 1:是                                                                                        |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| value           | 执行周期间隔                     | 是       | 单位为天，当cycle为1时生效                                                                       |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| cycle_run_time  | 周期任务在执行当日的具体执行时间 | 否       | 当cycle为1时必填，格式：09:00:00                                                                 |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| task_start_time | 开始时间                         | 是       | 格式：2023-03-21 14:27:45                                                                        |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| system_userids  | 邮件发送对象用户id               | 否       | 数组格式                                                                                         |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
| custom_email    | 邮件发送对象-系统外用户邮箱      | 否       | 字符串格式，多个邮箱使用 ``\n`` 分隔                                                             |      |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+
|| file_type      || 文档拆分                        || 否      || user: 报告按照系统用户进行拆分（严格按照定义角色权限生成）                                      ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || user_by_group: 报告按照系统用户进行拆分（不按照定义角色权限生成，而是按照资产登记的结果来生成） ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || group：按照资产组进行拆分                                                                       ||     |
||                ||                                 ||         || 空值: 不进行拆分                                                                                ||     |
+-----------------+----------------------------------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+


请求示例
+++++++++
   
.. code-block:: python

    import requests
    import json

    url = "http://127.0.0.1:38202/api/v1/report/map/config"

    payload = json.dumps({
    "name": "TEST_API_REPORT1",
    "type": "docx",
    "task_ids": [
        112
    ],
    "group_ids": [
        7350,
        7358
    ],
    "condition": [
        "domain"
    ],
    "cycle": 0,
    "value": "0",
    "cycle_run_time": "09:00:00",
    "task_start_time": "2024-02-13 14:46:37",
    "system_userids": [
        1
    ],
    "custom_email": "test1@test.cn\ntest2@test.cn",
    "file_type": "group"
    })
    headers = {
    'Content-Type': 'application/json',
    'Cookie': 'sessionid=550q0nyl2gijkeqxhu2kibep5wgdxrin'
    }

    response = requests.request("POST", url, headers=headers, data=payload)

    print(response.text)
